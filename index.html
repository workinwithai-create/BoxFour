BoxFour

Programming drums is tedious; boxing them takes ten seconds. Type your BPM and 4 chords, put on headphones, and beatbox 4 bars over a real band. BoxFour finds every hit, sorts it into kick, snare or hat by how bright it sounds, snaps it to your grid and plays it back on a real acoustic kit (Virtuosity Drums, CC0). Every loop pass is a new take. Export a WAV and a drum MIDI file (GM kit, one track per take) that sit on bar 1. Nothing is uploaded, no AI sounds.

Box it

Headphones on, so the band doesn't leak into the mic. One bar of count-in, then box: "b" or "boom" for kick, "pf" or "k" for snare, "ts" for hat. Recording loops until you stop it, and every 4-bar pass becomes its own take. Detection runs on this device.

Hits darker than the first split play the kick, brighter than the second play the hi-hat, the rest play the snare. Auto-sort learns the splits from your own takes.

Play along live

Velocity works from a MIDI controller; the sustain pedal (CC64) holds piano, bass and pad notes. Notes never start or stop the band. Computer keys: 1 2 3 = kick snare hat; with a pitched instrument, A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play C to C and Z / X change octave.
