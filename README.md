# BoxFour

**Beatbox it over your chords and get a real drum kit plus drum MIDI.** Programming drums one click at a time is slow. Beatboxing them takes ten seconds. Type your BPM, key and 4 chords, put on headphones, and box 4 bars over a real band. BoxFour finds every hit in the browser and sorts it into kick, snare or hi-hat by how bright it sounds. It snaps the hits to your grid and plays them back on a real acoustic kit. Every loop pass becomes a new take.

- **Your loop first:** BPM 60–180, any of the 12 keys, and a 4-chord entry such as `Am7 Dm7 G Cmaj7`. These settings are used for playback and for every export, and they persist in localStorage. The presets are only demos.
- **Looping recorder:** Press ● Record to get a one-bar count-in. The loop then runs until you stop it, and **each 4-bar pass becomes its own take** (Take 1, 2, …). Each take can be set to Loop or Silent, muted, renamed, edited or deleted. By default the newest take loops and older ones go silent; tick "Layer" to keep stacking. Takes persist in localStorage, up to 16. Hits from the pads or a MIDI pad controller during recording land in the same take. If you don't allow the mic, BoxFour records pads and MIDI only.
- **How hits are sorted:** onsets come from log spectral flux, and sorting uses the power-weighted spectral centroid of each hit. There are two split sliders (kick/snare and snare/hat) and an **Auto-sort** button that learns the splits from your own takes (deterministic 1-D k-means). You can tap grid cells to add or remove hits.
- **Recipes:** Snap to 16ths, Your feel (no quantize), Lock the backbeat, Add a hat bed. The recipe is one pure function, and it feeds live playback, the WAV and the MIDI alike.
- **Input / output:** pick the mic (audio input select, with echo cancellation, noise suppression and AGC off). Output and input latency are compensated, and there's a manual trim in ms. You can also load a beatbox voice memo. Pick the audio output device too (`AudioContext.setSinkId` where supported; otherwise the app says it uses the system output).
- **Web MIDI in/out:** device select is persisted. **Play along live**: notes play the kit right away using the GM drum map (36 kick, 38 snare, 42 hat; other notes map by pitch class). You can switch to electric piano, finger bass or string pad, all with velocity and sustain (CC64). Notes never start the transport. MIDI Start/Stop runs it only if you opt in. Pads, keys 1/2/3 and on-screen keys work as a fallback. MIDI out sends the kit on ch10, bass on ch1, electric piano on ch2 and pad on ch3.
- **Chairs (real samples, pinned):**
  - acoustic kit (kick, snare, closed hi-hat) from Virtuosity Drums, CC0: `sfzinstruments/virtuosity_drums@9f04cf9a734527edfbb0a4eee1f674e45bbf71bc`
  - finger bass, electric piano and string ensemble from FluidR3_GM (Frank Wen, CC BY 3.0): `gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts@044fab8e1456bfafc5776e86dfd6bb8697149aef`
  - Every bass, piano and pad semitone is sampled, so there are 0 pitch shifts (the engine refuses anything over ±4 st). If any sample fails, the app names the missing instruments and disables play, record and export.
- **Export:** `boxfour-<recipe>-<bpm>bpm-<key>.wav` is 48 kHz, 24-bit stereo. The first downbeat is at sample 0 and the length is exact (`bars*4*60/bpm*48000`). The ring-out tail is folded back into the start, compressor latency is removed, and the peak is −1.1 dBFS. The matching `.mid` is format 1 with tempo and 4/4, one track per chair, and **one GM-drum track per take** (ch10). "Export takes" downloads a .zip of per-take WAV stems (kit only, exact length).
- **Timing:** AudioContext lookahead scheduler (LOOKAHEAD_MS 25, SCHEDULE_AHEAD 0.12). Live changes to BPM, key, chords, recipe, mutes and take mode land on the next step. Hiding the tab stops cleanly.
